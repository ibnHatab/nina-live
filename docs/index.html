Nina

A voice assistant that lives in your home or office, on your own device.

Nina is open-source software (nina-live) that runs on a small computer you own, such as a Raspberry Pi with a microphone array and speakers. You talk to her; she answers in her own voice, keeps your calendar and to-do list, plays the radio, sets timers, looks things up, and runs the home or the office through Home Assistant.

What she decides to do is governed by rules you can read (the Metis rule language), not only by a language model: a model understands what you say, the rules decide what happens.

What she connects to

  • Home Assistant, on the same device: your lights, ventilation, music, calendars and lists.
  • Google Calendar and Google Tasks, through Home Assistant, when the device's owner signs in with their Google account: to read your agenda and to add or change the events and tasks you ask for.
  • Google's Gemini models, to hear and understand what you say and to answer.

Your data

Her memory (an event log) and Home Assistant's sign-ins stay on your device. There is no server of ours in between. Read the privacy policy.

For developers

The code, the domain rules and the installation path are on GitHub: nina-live (her core), fsttm-live (her voice and turn-taking), acasa (Home Assistant and the installation), and her architecture.
